MAINTAIN YOUR DIRECTORY

How to Use and Maintain This Directory

Keep it somewhere visible and make sure a trusted person knows where it is.

1
✍️

Complete It

Write names, phone numbers, addresses, allergies and emergency contacts in full.

2
✅

Verify It

Call the pharmacy, clinic and hospital to confirm details are correct before an emergency.

3
📤

Share It

Give a copy to a family member or caregiver — with the senior's permission.

4
🔄

Update It

Review after every medicine or contact change — and at least every few months.

5
🔒

Keep It Private

Store personal and medical details securely. Share only with trusted people.

💡 Pro Tip: Keep a printed copy near the phone, and a photo of the key contact page on a trusted caregiver's smartphone. The SOS button uses your saved contact for instant WhatsApp alerts.
