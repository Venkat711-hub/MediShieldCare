SOS SYSTEM

How the SOS Emergency System Works

One button sends your exact location to emergency services and your family. Here's what happens when you tap SOS.

🆘

Press the Red SOS Button — Bottom Right

The floating red SOS button is on every page of this website. Press it at any time for immediate help.

📡

Step 1: Location Detection

The SOS system uses your device's GPS to get your precise coordinates and street address automatically.

📞

Step 2: One-Tap Calling

Direct call buttons for 112, 108, Police, and the Senior Citizen Helpline — one tap connects you.

💬

Step 3: WhatsApp Alert

Your GPS location + address is automatically composed into a WhatsApp message to your saved emergency contact.

🗺️

Step 4: Maps Link

Share a Google Maps link showing your exact location so family, friends, or emergency responders can navigate to you.

📲 Save your emergency contact in the My Directory page. The SOS system will automatically use that number for WhatsApp alerts.
IN AN EMERGENCY

Follow These 4 Steps

Keep calm, act quickly and share clear information with responders.

1
📞

Call for Help

In India, call 112 for integrated emergency assistance. Ambulance number 108 is also available in many states; confirm local availability.

2
📍

Share the Location

Give the full address, nearest landmark, your phone number and the best way to enter the building.

3
🗣️

Explain the Situation

Say what happened, when it started, the person's age and any known allergies or major medical conditions.

4
📋

Keep Information Ready

Keep the medicine list, ID, medical records and emergency contact details nearby at all times.

⚠️ Critical: If the person is unconscious, has severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, or signs of stroke — call 112 immediately without delay.
