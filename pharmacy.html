TIPS

Smart Pharmacy Tips for Seniors

Simple habits that help seniors get the most from pharmacy services safely.

01
Always carry your medicine list

Show it to every pharmacist, doctor and specialist — including when visiting a new provider.

02
Ask about generics (Jan Aushadhi)

Generic medicines contain the same active ingredient at significantly lower cost. Government Jan Aushadhi stores offer 50–90% lower prices. Find your nearest at janaushadhi.gov.in.

03
Always check the expiry date

Before purchasing and before taking — always check the expiry date printed on the package.

04
Ask about home delivery

Many pharmacies offer regular home delivery for prescriptions. Ask your pharmacist and arrange a standing order for your monthly medicines.

05
Confirm before switching brands

Even if medicines appear similar, do not switch brands without asking your doctor or pharmacist first. Formulations can differ.
