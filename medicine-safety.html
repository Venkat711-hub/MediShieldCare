💊 MEDICATION SAFETY

Medicine Safety
Review the List Regularly

Taking multiple medications can be complex. A regular review with your doctor or pharmacist helps keep you safe. Here's everything you need to know.

📋 Why Review? ✅ Daily Habits
MEDICATION REVIEW

Why Seniors Should Review Their Medication List

A doctor or pharmacist can help check whether every medicine is still needed and appropriate for your current health.

🗓️

1. Outdated Medications

Expired or unused drugs can lose their effectiveness or become unsafe. Remove expired medicines safely — do not use them past their expiry date.

⚡

2. Duplicate Ingredients

Different brands may contain the same active ingredient, leading to accidental overdose. Always check with your pharmacist before combining medicines.

⚠️

3. Harmful Interactions

Prescription medicines, over-the-counter drugs and supplements can interact. Some interactions reduce effectiveness; others can be dangerous.

📈

4. Changing Doses

As your body and health change with age, your dosages may need adjustment. Kidney and liver function significantly affect how medicines are processed.

👨‍⚕️

5. Keep All Providers Informed

Ensure every doctor, specialist and pharmacist you see has the exact same medication list — including vitamins, supplements and over-the-counter drugs.

🚫 Never start, stop or change a dose without advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

DAILY HABITS

Simple Daily Medicine-Safety Habits

Use a routine that is easy for the senior and caregiver to follow every day.

📋

Keep One Updated List

Include medicine name, strength, dose, time and reason for taking it. Share with every healthcare provider.

⏰

Use a Reminder System

A pill organiser, calendar, or phone alarm can support the routine and help avoid missed doses.

🏷️

Follow the Label

Take medicines exactly as prescribed. Never share medicines with another person — even if symptoms seem similar.

🔔

Report Side Effects Promptly

Contact the doctor or pharmacist if any new symptom appears after taking a medicine — dizziness, confusion, rash or others.

🗄️

Store Medicines Safely

Keep in original packaging, in a cool dry place, away from heat, moisture, sunlight and children.

👩‍⚕️

Tell All Your Doctors

Tell every doctor and specialist about all medicines — including vitamins, supplements and herbal remedies.

🛒

Order Online with Care

Before purchasing from an online pharmacy, ask your doctor if the company is legitimate and licensed by CDSCO.

💉

Don't Skip Immunizations

Several vaccines are recommended for seniors (flu, pneumonia, shingles). Ask your doctor for a vaccination schedule.

MEDICINES. MANAGED. SAFELY.

Keep your medicine list updated and reviewed regularly with your doctor.
